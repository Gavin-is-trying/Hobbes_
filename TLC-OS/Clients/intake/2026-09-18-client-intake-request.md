## Privacy and Safety Check

The website publishes every document under `TLC-OS/`. Do not commit real customer
names, phone numbers, email addresses, or street addresses to this repository or to
a published client list. The browser form keeps entries in the page only; a durable
system of record must be selected and approved before any personal data is stored.
