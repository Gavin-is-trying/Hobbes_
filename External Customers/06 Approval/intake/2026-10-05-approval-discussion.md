## Current approval paths described

### Customer approves through Jobber

Gavin described a customer approving the quote in Jobber, leading to the job and scheduling step.

The exact UI statuses, conversion actions, and any automatic behavior have not been independently verified.

### Customer replies through another channel

A customer may instead approve by email, text, or iMessage. The team then manually approves the quote in Jobber.

This records the reported workflow. It does not decide what a particular message legally accepts, establish a new proof-of-approval rule, or authorize recording approval for an actual customer.

## Desired future prerequisite: card on file

Gavin wants a card on file to become a prerequisite for **all new customers**, and ultimately **existing customers**, with rare exceptions.

This is a desired future state, not a claim that the requirement is currently active or an instruction to apply it now. The exceptions and any rollout for existing customers have not been defined.

Gavin described a Jobber setting that he believes can prevent quote acceptance without a card on file. **That capability and the current setting were not verified or enabled.**

### Payment method and charge consent are separate

These notes distinguish:

- Having a payment method stored
- The customer's authorization for recurring charges

The discussion does not supply consent wording, authorize storing card details, authorize a charge, or establish that existing customers have accepted a new requirement. No existing agreement or payment setting is changed.

## Pre-service safeguards to explore

Gavin wants to brainstorm what both parties should understand and agree to before service begins. This connects to the existing request in the Offer discussion for simple, clear TLC Terms of Service.

Topics raised for exploration include:

- The selected service scope, visit counts, and equal monthly payments

- Weather interruptions and missed-visit reconciliation

These are **discussion options, not newly adopted terms or conditions**. The selected scope and annual-count descriptions, as well as the confirmed extra-visit approval principle, remain in the Estimate notes. How those points should appear in a pre-service acceptance flow is still to be developed.

The missed-visit settlement method remains **expressly open** in the Offer notes. Nothing here selects automatic refunds, future credits, or another default.
