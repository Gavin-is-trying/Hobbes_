## Automated reminders: recalled timing

Gavin recalled automatic reminders at approximately **3, 7, and 14 days**, generally when there has been no request for changes, contact, or approval.

The timing is uncertain. **The actual cadence, triggering conditions, and stop conditions have not been verified.** The approximate numbers should not be treated as confirmed settings, a new customer promise, or an instruction to configure reminders.

Gavin described automation as the main follow-up path, with relatively little manual outbound follow-up in ordinary cases.

## When personal follow-up happens

### An estimate appears unread

An unread estimate may prompt a call to check that the email address and phone number are correct. Gavin said incorrect contact details have occurred before.

This records a reason he may investigate, not a rule to call every unread estimate or a claim that the unread status identifies the cause.

### Higher-priority Comanche Trace prospects

Gavin said Tier 1 and Tier 2 Comanche Trace prospects may merit personal outreach **before 14 days**, to ask about questions or hesitation.

The tier language refers to the provisional location priorities in the linked Leads discussion. The outreach decision is **case by case**. No fixed calling deadline, number of attempts, or promise to contact every such prospect was established.

This answer described prioritization. It did **not** resolve when an opportunity should be closed.

## Assistant caution about view status

The assistant noted that a view is not proof of interest, and an unread indication is not proof of delivery failure.

These are interpretation cautions. They do not establish an automated scoring rule, require a particular outreach action, or resolve the opportunity's status.
