## Two complementary parts of assessment

Gavin described **onsite work** and **offsite research** as complementary parts of an assessment. These are separate from whether the customer is present or absent.

### Onsite work

Gavin gathers information such as:

- Photos and videos
- Property features and quirks
- Gates and equipment access
- Grass types
- Dogs and relevant access considerations
- Questions that need answers to understand the work

He said he **does not measure lawns onsite**.

These points describe the work discussed. They are not a new mandatory field list or a claim that every item is collected in every case.

### Offsite research

Gavin described using:

- Street View
- Address-based online research
- MeasureMyLawn, drawing a shape to obtain square footage used for fertilization pricing

The order of offsite research relative to the onsite visit has **not been established**. No fixed before-or-after sequence is implied.

## Customer present or absent is a separate distinction

An assessment may be conducted with the customer present or absent, as described in the linked Leads notes. Customer attendance does not replace the onsite/offsite distinction.

When the customer is present, the conversation can explore:

- The customer's needs
- Whether the customer and provider are a mutual fit
- Expectations of the provider
- Occasionally, the general Offer

Gavin does not describe giving a detailed onsite presentation. The Offer may come up naturally; it is not a required presentation gate.
